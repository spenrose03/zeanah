Virtual Tours

The college has several cutting-edge research centers and labs available to undergraduate students, including the ones to the right.

SynDaver Lab

Join two of our biomedical engineering students Ifedayo and Jenna on a 360-degree tour of the Syndaver Lab in Dougherty Hal. The lab was the first ever for a US biomedical engineering program!

High Bay Lab

Join students Leah and Lauren for a 360-degree tour of the High Bay Lab—part of the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering in the John D. Tickle Building at the University of Tennessee.

Hydraulics and
Sedimentation Lab

Lauren and Leah, students in civil and environmental engineering, lead you on a tour of UT’s Hydraulics Lab. Inside the lab, they demonstrate the use of an open channel flume, a seepage box, and a centrifugal pump.

EcoCar HQ

Tickle College of Engineering EcoCar team members Dennis, Ramin, and Joseph lead a 360-degree tour of the EcoCar lab in the Department of Mechanical, Aerospace, and Biomedical Engineering at UT.
